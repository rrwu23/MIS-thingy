Pay rent. Every student of the signed-in admin is listed in the box below, in alphabetical order. Tick the students the rent is to be charged to — a click on a row ticks it, and a click on the same row again unticks it — and press Pay rent. The students ticked are put in front of you once more, with the backend's own rent named in the question, and on a Y every one of them is charged it, one student at a time. Only a signed-in admin's students can be charged, so the page asks the backend for permission as it loads — the button stays grey until that is answered — and asks again before anything is sent. A student the backend wrote a row for is unticked as soon as that is known, so a second press cannot charge them a second time; a student it wrote no row for stays ticked and is named under the box, so the next press tries only them. Nothing is kept between visits: a tick is a charge, and a charge that outlived the page could be sent twice.
