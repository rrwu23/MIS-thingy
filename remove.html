Remove student. Type the name of the student to take away and press remove. The name is read back to you once more, and the question is asked before anything happens: Yes takes the student away for good — it cannot be undone — and No leaves them alone. Nothing is done while the question stands. The backend has no route that takes a student away yet, so an answered Yes is met with that, and the account stays where it is.
