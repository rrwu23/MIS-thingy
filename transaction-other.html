Other transaction. For the ones that are not on a list. Type the points and the broad reason yourself — a minus in front of the amount takes points away instead of giving them — and put anything else worth remembering about the transaction in the memo. The date box is filled from the clock as the page loads and can be typed over, so a transaction can be filed under the day it happened. Nothing is written until the Y/N question is answered with Y, and the row is then filed under the broad reason and its points as its type, as Lost library book (-25 pts) shows.
