Transaction history. Student: … Every transaction the backend has recorded for this student — the date, the amount, what it was for, any memo, and the balance the account ended on. Nothing here is added up: each ending balance is the figure that transaction carries, and a field the backend does not send is shown as a dash. To edit a transaction, click it: its date, its amount, its type and its memo open in boxes and that row’s delete button becomes save and cancel. The delete button takes the row away, and the ending balance is not among the boxes — it is the backend’s own figure for the account. Neither can be brought back, so each is asked about once more before anything is sent.
