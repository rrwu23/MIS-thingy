Transaction. Pick the student the transaction is for from the box below — every student of the signed-in admin is listed there, and a click on a row picks that student while a second click on the same row unpicks them. The students picked together are remembered as sharing the one transaction the flow is about to enter: one key per picked student, every one of them carrying the same mark, with the whole pick kept in one key beside them. Only a signed-in admin may open a transaction, so the page asks the backend for permission as it loads, lists the students only once that permission is given, and asks it again about the picked students when you press continue.
